Tanzania’s richest music stars, ranked

Ten artists whose estimated fortunes lead the Bongo scene, with the range of figures reported and how we reached the order.

By the BongoPlanet editors · Updated 25 October 2026
How to read this list. Celebrity net worth is never audited or officially published, so every figure here is an estimate drawn from public reports, and the people named have not confirmed them. Where sources disagree we show the full range. Ranks 2 to 5 are close, and the order reflects how often reports place each artist higher.

How we compiled this

We compared several published rankings of Tanzanian and East African musicians, noted each reported figure, and ranked by the range they point to and how consistently an artist appears near the top. We left out figures that appeared in only one place when they conflicted with the rest. Figures are in US dollars. We do not accept payment to change a ranking.
