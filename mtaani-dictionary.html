The Mtaani Dictionary

Street Swahili from Dar es Salaam, explained simply. Learn the words you hear in songs, films and on the corner.

Sayings you will hear

  • Pole pole ndio mwendoSlowly, slowly is the way to go. Patience wins.
  • Haba na haba hujaza kibabaLittle by little fills the measure. Small steps add up.
  • Haraka haraka haina barakaHurry, hurry has no blessing. Rushing spoils things.
  • Umoja ni nguvuUnity is strength.

Know a word we missed?

Street language changes fast and every neighbourhood has its own flavour. Send us a word and we will add it, with credit if you like.

Sources and notes

  • Uta Reuster-Jahn and Roland Kießling, Lugha ya Mitaani in Tanzania (Swahili Forum 13, 2006), a study with a dictionary of about 1,100 street words.
  • Uta Reuster-Jahn, From slang to sleek: changing language attitudes of urban youths in Tanzania (2016).
  • B. O. A. Oheda, Swahili slang, University of Dar es Salaam (1995).

Mtaani slang shifts by area, age and year, so a word may carry a different shade where you live. Some words are casual and best kept for friends. Spot a mistake? Email iamisaacjob@gmail.com.
