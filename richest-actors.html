Tanzania’s richest actors, ranked

Ten Bongo Movie stars named most often as the screen’s top earners, with the range of figures reported.

By the BongoPlanet editors · Updated 25 October 2026
Read this first. Net worth is never audited or officially published, and the reports we found for actors are weaker than those for musicians. The same names appear in the same order across lists dated 2022 to 2025, which suggests figures are being recycled rather than freshly researched. Treat this as a snapshot of what is publicly reported, not a financial record. The people named have not confirmed any figure.

What about comedians?

We did not rank them. We found no credible published estimates for Tanzanian comedians, only pages that were clearly low quality, and we would rather leave a list out than print numbers we cannot stand behind. Idris Sultan, who works as an actor, comedian and radio host, appears above. The Orijino Komedi cast, including Masanja Mkandamizaji, Joti and Mpoki, are among the best known names in Tanzanian comedy. If you have a reliable source on comedy earnings, send it to us.

How we compiled this

We compared three published rankings of Tanzanian actors, recorded the lowest and highest figure reported for each person, and ordered the list by how consistently each name is placed. Ranks 6 and 7 swap places in one report. Figures are in US dollars. We do not accept payment to change a ranking.
